## §1 Summary

To: Operations Controller. From: Procurement. Date: 12 June 2023. Subject: Steel index movement and impact on Drive Assemblies. The benchmark steel index rose 18% quarter-on-quarter, effective for June deliveries under our indexed supply contract.

## §2 Index Movement and Cost Impact

The 18% increase in the steel index feeds directly into Drive Assemblies unit cost, which carries the highest steel content of our product lines. The estimated effect on June cost of goods sold for Drive Assemblies is an increase of approximately USD 495,000 versus the frozen budget. Precision Fittings and Aftermarket Parts are minimally affected.
