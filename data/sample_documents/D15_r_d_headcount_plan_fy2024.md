## §1 Plan Overview

To: R&D Finance Lead. From: R&D Programme Office. Date: 6 May 2024. Subject: R&D headcount ramp for the platform programme. The approved plan expands R&D engineering headcount in CC-5100 from 12 to 25 across the second and third quarters, ahead of the platform launch.

## §3 Hiring Schedule and Cost

Hiring begins in May 2024. Because the annual budget was frozen at the prior headcount of 12, the ramp creates an operating-expense variance in CC-5100 that grows month by month. The May effect is approximately USD 281,000 above the frozen budget as the first cohort onboards.
