## §1 Contract Status

To: Commercial Director. From: Sales Operations. Date: 8 November 2023. Subject: Non-renewal of Tier-1 OEM supply agreement. Our largest North America Drive Assemblies OEM customer did not renew its annual supply agreement, effective November. This customer represented roughly 22% of North America Drive Assemblies volume.

## §2 Revenue Impact

The non-renewal reduces North America Drive Assemblies revenue by an estimated USD 743,000 in November versus budget, driven almost entirely by lost volume rather than price. Recovery actions with two replacement accounts are in progress but will not offset November.
