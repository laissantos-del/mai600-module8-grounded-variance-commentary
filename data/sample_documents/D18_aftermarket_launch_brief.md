## §2 Launch Plan

To: Commercial Director. From: Product. Date: 18 June 2025. Subject: Aftermarket range launch, planning basis. The Aftermarket Parts range launches in August. The planning basis assumes an incremental USD 620,000 of revenue in the launch month, with associated launch costs of approximately USD 400,000 in marketing and channel support.

## §4 Planning Caveat

These figures reflect the launch scope agreed in June. Any subsequent change to the launch scope, channel mix, or promotional calendar will change the revenue and cost profile, and this brief will not be reissued. Confirm the current scope with the Product team before relying on these figures for reporting.
