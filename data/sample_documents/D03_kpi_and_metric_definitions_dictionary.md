## Gross Margin

Gross margin is revenue less cost of goods sold, expressed as a percentage of revenue. Cost of goods sold includes direct material, direct labour, and allocated manufacturing overhead.

## Price, Volume and Mix Effects

A revenue variance is decomposed into a price effect (change in realised selling price at constant volume and mix), a volume effect (change in units sold at constant price and mix), and a mix effect (change in the composition of products sold). The three effects sum to the total revenue variance.

## Net Leverage

Net leverage is net debt divided by trailing twelve-month EBITDA. Net debt is total borrowings less cash and cash equivalents. The lending covenant limits net leverage to 3.5x, tested quarterly.
