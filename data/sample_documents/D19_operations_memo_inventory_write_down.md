## §1 Write-Down Basis

To: Operations Controller. From: Operations. Date: 9 October 2025. Subject: Slow-moving inventory write-down. A review of aged inventory identified slow-moving Drive Assemblies and Aftermarket stock carried above net realisable value. A write-down of approximately USD 1,800,000 is recognised in October cost of goods sold.

## §2 Cash and Ratio Effect

The write-down is a non-cash charge that increases October cost of goods sold and reduces reported days inventory outstanding. It does not affect the revolver draw or covenant headroom for the quarter.
