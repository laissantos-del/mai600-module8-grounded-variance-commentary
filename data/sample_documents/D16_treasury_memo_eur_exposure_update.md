## §1 Exposure Summary

To: Group Financial Controller. From: Treasury. Date: 9 December 2024. Subject: EUR exposure and the December reporting impact. EMEA revenue is invoiced in euro and translated to US dollars at the average monthly rate. The euro weakened approximately 9% against the dollar during the month.

## §2 Rate Movement and Revenue Impact

The 9% adverse rate movement reduces reported EMEA revenue by approximately USD 405,000 in December versus the frozen budget, which assumed the prior-year average rate. The movement is translation only and does not reflect a change in underlying EMEA volume or pricing. No hedge was in force for the period.
